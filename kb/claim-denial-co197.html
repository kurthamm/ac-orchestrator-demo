Claim Denial Code CO-197: Precertification/Authorization Absent

CO-197 means the service required prior authorization and none was on file. Member is not liable when an in-network provider failed to obtain authorization. Next steps for agents:

  • Confirm the servicing provider is in-network. If so, advise the member they should not pay the bill.
  • Open a claim reprocessing request; the provider may submit a retro-authorization within 60 days of the denial.
