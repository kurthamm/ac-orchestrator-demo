Changing Your Primary Care Provider (PCP)

Members may change their PCP at any time at no cost. Rules:

  • The new PCP must be in-network for the member's plan and accepting new patients.
  • Changes requested on or before the 20th of the month take effect the 1st of the following month; after the 20th, the 1st of the second following month.
  • If the current PCP is retiring or leaving the network, the change takes effect immediately.
  • A new member ID card is mailed automatically within 7–10 business days.
